A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

things up.

Ask us something else ↗
Is this only for restaurants?+

No. We work on solutions for local businesses across different industries. The restaurant example is one way to demonstrate the workflow. Your project starts with your business needs.

Do I need all four services?+

No. Start with one useful improvement: a better website, an enquiry workflow, a voice agent or a focused marketing project. We’ll help you choose a sensible starting point.

Can you work with my existing tools?+

We’ll review them first. Integration depends on the tools and the access they provide. We confirm what’s possible and any third-party costs before agreeing on the scope.

How much does a project cost?+

Pricing depends on the scope, integrations and ongoing usage. Tell us what you need, and we’ll outline a proposal with setup costs and any recurring fees clearly separated.

Will an AI agent replace my team?+

The aim is to give your team useful backup. We design around the questions and tasks it should handle, with clear limits and a handoff for situations that need a person.
